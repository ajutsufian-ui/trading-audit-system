System risk rules (hard-coded in production engine)

  • Risk per trade: 0.5% of equity, stop-loss on every trade
  • Stop distance: 2 × ATR(14), trailing — never loosened
  • Day stop: no new trades after −2% day P/L
  • Kill-switch: full halt at −5% drawdown
  • Float cap: worst position force-closed at −1.5% equity
  • Concurrency: max 2 positions; correlation gate 0.7 blocks correlated entries

What makes this auditable

  • Every row shows the stop that existed at entry — losses can't hide
  • Entries sized by risk (fixed fraction of equity), so sizing is checkable
  • Signals execute at next bar open — no look-ahead backtest bias
  • Costs (fees + slippage) are modeled in results, not ignored
  • Same rules enforced in live engine and backtest — one source of truth
